## 👨‍💻 About me

iOS developer writing Swift since 2017 — UIKit and SwiftUI, MVVM and TCA, RxSwift and async/await. In 2026 I started working as a Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform developer too.

- 🔭 **Building:** [**Stillplace**](https://stillplace.app) — a calm app for finding your quiet place
- 🎮 **Building:** [**Around Warsaw**](https://aroundwarsaw.app) — a free, open-source isometric arcade drive (and helicopter) through the real centre of Warsaw, made from OpenStreetMap and GUGiK open data
- 🌱 **Learning:** Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, game dev with Godot
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Swift, UIKit, SwiftUI, TCA, RxSwift, Swift concurrency, KMP and CMP
